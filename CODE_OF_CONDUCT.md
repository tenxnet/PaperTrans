# Code of Conduct

PaperTrans welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect private data, unpublished research, credentials, and copyrighted material shared by mistake.
- Give appropriate credit for code, research, design, and documentation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, repeated disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens contributors or the project. Reports should be made privately to the maintainer through the security or contact channel published by the repository. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-affiliated community spaces.
